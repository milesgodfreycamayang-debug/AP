SDG 5
SDG 5 aims to end all forms of discrimination against women and girls everywhere. It seeks to prevent violence, trafficking, child marriage, and other forms of exploitation while ensuring equal opportunities in education, employment, healthcare, and leadership. Achieving gender equality helps promote economic growth, improve quality of life, and build a fair and inclusive society.
End discrimination against women and girls.

Prevent violence, trafficking, and child marriage.

Promote equal access to education, healthcare, and employment.

Increase women’s participation in leadership and decision-making.

Women currently hold 27.2% of parliamentary seats worldwide. At the current pace, equal representation in leadership could take 100–140 years.

SDG 6
SDG 6 aims to ensure the availability and sustainable management of clean water and sanitation for everyone. It focuses on providing safe drinking water, improving sanitation services, and protecting water resources. Clean water is essential for good health, disease prevention, and sustainable development.
Around 59 million Filipinos (53%) lack access to safely managed piped water.

Around 43 million Filipinos (37%) lack safely managed sanitation.

Water pollution and inadequate infrastructure remain major challenges.

Expanding access to clean water helps improve public health and quality of life.

SDG 7
SDG 7 aims to ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy for all. It promotes renewable energy, improved energy efficiency, and innovation to reduce dependence on fossil fuels. Clean energy supports economic growth, protects the environment, and helps address climate change.
92% of the world’s population has access to electricity.

Renewable energy capacity reached 544 watts per person in 2024.

$24.6 billion was invested in clean energy projects in developing countries.

The Philippines continues expanding solar, wind, and hydropower but still relies heavily on fossil fuels.
